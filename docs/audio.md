# Audio

Implementation: `crates/dusk_client/src/audio.rs` (Bevy systems) and `audio/custom.rs` (gaze
layer, proximity emitters, footsteps, greets); `crates/dusk_formats/src/sound.rs` (event sound
names, `sprite_sounds.txt`, voice lookup, name resolution) and `content/sounds.rs` (loader and
the list of every referenced sound).

Every sound is one of our files:

- effects: `assets/content/sfx/*.wav`, generated by `tools/sfxgen` (16-bit PCM
  mono, 44.1 kHz; see the README there);
- music: `assets/content/music/` (the soundtrack, MP3).

Sounds are named by bare file name and resolved through the client's file index; a name
without an extension is a `.wav` (`gaze_open` = `gaze_open.wav`; `sound::resolve_sound`).
`DUSK_AUDIO_LOG=1` logs every started sound and track at `info` level and warns once about
each name that does not resolve.

## Music and ambience

| Rule | |
|---|---|
| Music: the map's `music=` list (playable entries), else the whole soundtrack | DESIGN |
| Ambience: the map's `ambience=` list (one random entry loops); none by default | DESIGN |
| On a map change a track that is on the new list keeps playing, otherwise 2 s crossfade | DESIGN |
| A song plays once, then a random *other* track of the list starts | DESIGN |
| Ambience follows the music volume / toggle | DESIGN |

The map list comes from `content::maps` (`MapInfo.music`, `MapInfo.ambience`).

## Sound effects

All one-shots go through the `PlaySfx` message (`SfxAt::Ui` or `SfxAt::Unit(entity)`, optional
delay). Positional ones are attenuated linearly from full volume at 4 cells to silence at 18
cells and dropped beyond (no stereo panning). At most 24 run at once and the same file starts
at most once per frame. Names are the constants of `dusk_formats::sound::builtin`; arrays are
variants, one picked at random.

| Event | Sound |
|---|---|
| Melee hit/crit by an NPC | `HIT_UNARMED`: `hit_npc_1..4` |
| Melee hit/crit by the local player | edged weapon (axe, sword, dagger): `HIT_BLADE` `hit_blade_1..4`; mace, staff, wand: `HIT_BLUNT` `hit_blunt_1..4`; empty hand: `hit_npc_*` |
| Melee hit/crit by another player | `hit_blade_*` (their weapon is not known to the client) |
| Miss, Evade / Dodge / Parry | `miss` / `dodge` / `parry` |
| Block | `block_1..3` |
| Local player hurt | `player_hurt_1..3` |
| NPC swings | voice `attack` |
| NPC swings after ≥15 s without swinging | voice `aggro` (else `attack`) |
| NPC takes a melee hit | voice `hit` |
| NPC dies | voice `death` (not throttled) |
| `CastStart` | the casting kit's `sound` at the caster |
| `SpellGo` | go kit + traveling kit (if `travel_ms > 0`) sound at the caster; impact kit sound at up to 3 targets after `travel_ms` |
| Level up | `level_up` |
| Any UI `Button` pressed | `ui_click` |
| New target selected | `ui_target` |
| Window opened / closed (window manager, game menu) | `ui_window_open` / `ui_window_close` |
| Item used (the `item_use` kit) | `item_use` |

Voice throttle: one line per unit per 1.5 s (deaths exempt).

Kit sounds come from `data/spell_visuals.txt` (`sound=` of a `[kit <name>]`, bare names):
`spell_heavy_slash`, `spell_bleed_hit`, `spell_skull_crack`, `spell_dash`, `spell_blade_hit`,
`spell_ground_slam`, `spell_knife_throw`, `spell_fire_cast`, `spell_ember_whoosh`,
`spell_ember_burst`, `spell_hook_throw`, `spell_hook_hit`, `spell_bow_draw`,
`spell_arrow_release`, `spell_arrow_hit`, `spell_ember_heal`, `spell_veil`,
`spell_warden_sweep`, `spell_warden_roar`, `item_use`. Melee auto attacks get their sound from
the swing result above, so the `melee_hit` kit has none.

## NPC voices

`npc_<model>_<event>.wav` plus optional `_1`..`_4` variants (one picked at random), events
`aggro`, `attack`, `hit`, `death` and `greet` (`sound::voice_lines`). A model without files
falls back to its name prefix (`glarewolf_alpha` → `glarewolf`, `goblin_charger` → `goblin`).
Targeting an NPC that has a greet plays it at the NPC (once per 8 s per NPC). Voices exist for
`glarewolf`, `stooped`, `hollowed_warden`, `goblin`, `spider`, `antlion_small` and greets for
`cairnkeeper`, `lightworker`, `lowshade_guard`.

## Proximity loops

`assets/data/sprite_sounds.txt` (`sprite sound radius`; sprite = file stem, trailing `*`
= prefix; bare sound names are `.wav`): on map load every cell whose layers use a matching
sprite becomes an emitter, grouped per sound. Every `C` cell of the map's `.cover` also emits
`loop_cairn_fire` (radius 6). Each group plays one loop whose volume is `1 - d / radius` for
the nearest emitter `d` cells away, eased over 0.5 s and stopped when silent. Uses the effects
volume.

## Gaze layer

Three loops mixed on top of the map ambience while `gaze::GazeView.active` (maps with a
`.cover` sidecar), at the music/ambience volume, each eased at 1/1.5 s per unit of gain and
stopped when silent:

| Cover under the player | `amb_open_sky` | `amb_shelter` | `amb_eye_open` × openness |
|---|---|---|---|
| open sky | 1.0 | 0 | 1.0 |
| shade | 0.55 | 0.3 | 0.75 |
| deep shelter | 0.12 | 1.0 | 0.35 |
| cairn | 0.2 | 0.75 | 0.3 |

The sky wind is lowered by 30 % of the Eye level. There is no low-pass filter in Bevy's audio,
so "muffled" shelter is only the volume trick plus `amb_shelter` itself being dark.

Strain cues (non-positional, effects volume): from strain 40 `strain_heartbeat` repeats every
1.5 s (volume 0.35) down to every 0.6 s at 100 (volume 1.0); from 70 a `strain_whisper` or
`strain_breath` every 5–13 s (sooner and louder as strain rises; the first 3 s after crossing
70). The other cues (`gaze_open`, `gaze_close`, `gaze_spot_enter`, `strain_overwhelm`,
`cairn_*`, `quest_*`, `dialogue_open`, `hit_heavy`, stings) are triggered by the gaze, director,
dialogue, menu and feel code; `sound::builtin::CUES` lists them.

`DUSK_GAZE_FAKE=strain,cover,openness` (cover `open|shade|shelter|cairn` or `. s S C`) writes
`GazeView` every frame for testing without the server; `DUSK_GAZE_FAKE=cycle` walks the cover
kinds every 10 s while strain (10–100, 30 s) and openness (0–1, 20 s) sweep.

## Footsteps

The running local player plays `foot_dirt_N` (or `foot_stone_N` under deep shelter / at a
cairn) every 1.25 cells, at 45 % of the effects volume. NPCs have no footsteps.

## Settings

Defaults: music 15 %, effects 20 % (linear gain 0.15 / 0.20); the options menu sets master,
music and effects volumes. `DUSK_MUSIC_VOLUME` / `DUSK_SFX_VOLUME` (0..100) override the
defaults. Keys: `M` toggles music + ambience, `N` toggles effects (+ proximity loops); a short
notice is shown.

## Tests

- `dusk_formats/tests/sounds.rs`: every sound of `content::sounds::referenced` (event sounds
  and cues, kit sounds of `spell_visuals.txt`, `sprite_sounds.txt`, `maps.txt` music/ambience,
  the soundtrack, voices of every non-friendly model in `npc_templates.txt`) is one of our files
  with a valid WAV/OGG/MP3 header; the effects stay within the 16 MB budget.
- `dusk_client` `audio::tests::referenced_sounds_decode`: all of them decode with Bevy's decoders.
- `python -I tools/sfxgen/sfxgen.py --check`: peaks, loudness, DC, clicks, loop seams, budget.

## Known gaps

- No stereo panning / true spatial audio; listener is the player, not the camera.
- Footsteps do not depend on the terrain beyond the gaze cover (dirt / stone).
- Item and object sounds (pick up, equip, doors, chests) wait for those features.
- Casting sounds are not cut when a cast is interrupted.
- Spell misses and player deaths have no special sound.
