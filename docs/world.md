# Duskhollow: world bible

Duskhollow shares a world with *Dreadbound* (a third-person souls-like set in the hamlet of
Hollowmere). Same sky, same Eye, different corner of it and a different lens: Dreadbound follows
one wanderer, Duskhollow is the online view of many wanderers.

## The Eye

- Far up in the sky, ringed by godly rays reaching outward, as if chained to the sky dome. It sits
  over a **rift** torn into a **crimson sky**; the rift itself is pitch black.
- Said to be the remains of a celestial being, one of a few left after a disaster many years ago.
  The great swords that stand in the land (the sword barrows) are thought to be others.
- It **replaced the sun**. There is no day and no night, only red light and the Eye's moods.
- Only the eldest alive can barely recall a time before it.
- It has hung there for generations **half-lidded**. Lately it **opens wide** (the night the
  swords fell, in Hollowmere's telling). When it opens, beasts turn and the dead do not stay down.
  It never fully closes.
- Its gaze can be **felt**. It is exhausting, builds anxiety and despair, and over time
  **corrupts** a soul. Fights under it are to be avoided. The gaze also comes partly through the
  red sky itself, so shade helps but never ends it. **Nobody is ever fully rested.**
- It has strange powers, not all of them observed.

## People

- **Shelter is everything.** Towns hide under cliff overhangs, in gorges, under deep eaves and
  roofed lanes. Travellers carry portable cover (veils, hooded mantles, canopy poles, covered
  carts); all of it is awkward.
- **Fire blinds the Watcher.** Rest **cairns** (stone cairn + brazier) are kept burning; the fire
  "remembers" those who rest by it. The common remedy is an **ember draught** brewed over cairn fire.
- **Lightworkers.** Crops still need the light, so the poor work the open red fields: gaunt,
  squinting, worn hollow-eyed. The rich live in safe darkness (cellar estates, covered manors,
  fungus gardens).
- **Enders.** Rare people somewhat resistant to the Eye. They stand its gaze longer and sometimes
  escape corruption, but exposure still exhausts them. **Every player is an Ender**, which is why
  they can do what others cannot: travel and fight in the open. Villagers call them **swords**
  ("Another sword under the Eye").
- **Wardens.** Guards who stand a post for decades under the gaze. Eventually something else wears
  their armour. A hollowed Warden still keeps its post.
- **The hollowed.** People taken by the gaze. The **Stooped** are hollowed lightworkers who still
  shuffle through the fields with their sickles.

## Duskhollow (the vale)

A deep gorge whose cliffs keep it in permanent dusk, which counts as safety. Its people live in:

- **Lowshade**: a hamlet under the great north-west overhang. Deep shelter. A rest cairn burns in
  the middle. Elder **Ysolde**, the cairnkeeper, speaks for it.
- **The Red Fields**: open terraces east of Lowshade where lightworkers tend crimson grain under
  the Eye. Canopy shelters (wood frames + tarps) dot the rows. Eye-turned **glarewolves** hunt here.
- **The Glare Gate**: a ruined gatehouse at the north mouth of the vale, open to the sky. Beyond it
  lies **the Fallen Blade**, a giant sword driven into the earth. **Warden Corvin** stood at the
  gate for thirty-one years.

## Folklore

- "Don't meet its eye." Those who stare too long go hollow; they sit and stare back.
- "The Eye sees the proud first."
- "Fire blinds the Watcher." Keep to the firelight.
- "Water looks back." Still pools show the Eye even under a roof.
- Enders are said to be born in total darkness.
- The ground directly under the rift is dead; no one who walked there came back.

## Voice

Weary, terse, practical; dark humour, no exposition dumps. Short sentences. People talk about work,
cover and the Eye the way others talk about weather.

- "Wood won't split itself, Eye or no Eye."
- "Another sword under the Eye. Few walk in from the gorge any more."

## Art rules

- **Light**: crimson from above, near-black in shade. No sun, no blue daylight, no near-white.
- **Glow**: only fire glows (cairns, braziers, lanterns). The Eye's gaze reads as a pale crimson
  wash, not a white spotlight.
- **Palette**: rust, ochre, oxblood, bruised violet shadows, charcoal stone, dull bone. Keep albedo
  dark; dither instead of gradients.
- **Shapes**: deep eaves, overhangs, canopies, hunched silhouettes, heads bowed (or tilted up,
  for the hollowed).
