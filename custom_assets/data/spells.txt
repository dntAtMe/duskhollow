# Duskhollow player skills (entries >= 50000), merged into the game.db spell templates by server
# and client. Format: dusk_formats::custom::parse_spells (keys mirror spell_template columns).
#
# Quick reference (docs/combat.md):
#   effectN        school_damage | weapon_damage | apply_aura | heal | heal_pct | charge (or a number)
#   effectN_data   d1,d2,d3. Damage/heal: d2 = base `value`. Weapon damage: d2 = weapon %.
#                  Auras: d1 = aura type (1 periodic damage, 2 periodic heal, 3 mechanic,
#                  11 move speed %, 100 strain gain %), d2 = mechanic (7 snare, 8 stun), d3 = value.
#   effectN_target caster | hostile | area_src_hostile | friendly ...; effectN_radius in cells.
#   Periodic auras: the formula is the total over `duration`, ticking every `interval` ms.
#   range: 64 per cell (130 = melee). speed: projectile (16 ~ 12 cells/s), 0 = instant.
#   Tooltip tokens: $E1min $E1max (effect values), $E2D3 (raw data3), $DUR, $INVL.
#   Visuals: visual=<legacy spell entry> plus *_kit=<spell_visual_kit id> overrides (0 = none);
#   go_anim / cast_anim: swing | cast | shoot | cast_alt.
#   Icons: tools/artgen/icons.py --custom-spells -> content/custom/icons/spells/.
#
# Balance (level 1, starter gear): legacy Fireball ~28 fire on a 1.5 s cast / 6 s cooldown,
# Mighty Blow 135% weapon / 7 s. Glarewolves have 42 hp, the Alpha 150, Corvin 180 x boss.
# Skills here stay at or under that line: they add choices (reach, control, sustain), not burst.
