-- Words for an Ender under the Eye --
WASD walks. Left-click marks a foe; right-click strikes it.
Tab picks the next foe in sight; 1-0, - and = cast.
C character, I pack, P abilities, J journal.
Click a pouch on the fallen to loot; click the living to talk.
Esc lets go of one thing at a time, then opens the menu.
M hushes the music, N the rest. Enter speaks.
Open sky wears on you; shade slows the strain, cairns clear it.
/say <words> speaks aloud, /help repeats these words.
