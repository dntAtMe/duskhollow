# Every spell of the game (dusk_formats::content::spells): player skills (50001..), auto
# attacks (50100, 50101), item spells (50110..), NPC spells (51001..).
#
# Quick reference (docs/combat.md):
#   effectN        school_damage | weapon_damage | apply_aura | heal | heal_pct | restore_mana_pct
#                  | melee_atk | ranged_atk | charge (or a number)
#   effectN_data   d1,d2,d3. Damage/heal: d2 = base `value`. Weapon damage: d2 = weapon %.
#                  Auras: d1 = aura type (1 periodic damage, 2 periodic heal, 3 mechanic,
#                  10 periodic mana, 11 move speed %, 14 damage dealt %, 15 damage taken %,
#                  100 strain gain %), d2 = mechanic (7 snare, 8 stun, 9 incapacitate), d3 = value.
#   effectN_target caster | hostile | area_src_hostile | area_dst_hostile | friendly ...;
#                  effectN_radius in cells.
#   Periodic auras: the formula is the total over `duration`, ticking every `interval` ms,
#   unless the description says "every" (then the value is per tick).
#   range: 64 per cell (130 = melee). speed: projectile (16 ~ 12 cells/s), 0 = instant.
#   abilities_tab: 1 Spells (default), 0 Actions.
#   Tooltip tokens: $E1min $E1max (effect values), $E2D3 (raw data3), $DUR, $INVL.
#   Visuals: data/spell_visuals.txt.
#   Icons: tools/artgen/icons.py --custom-spells -> content/custom/icons/spells/.
#
# Balance (level 1, starter gear): a fire bolt does ~28 on a 1.5 s cast / 6 s cooldown, a big
# swing 135% weapon / 7 s. Glarewolves have 42 hp, the Alpha 150, Corvin 180 x boss.
# Skills here stay at or under that line: they add choices (reach, control, sustain), not burst.
